# M Grow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

M Grow is published by {{DEVELOPER}}. This policy explains what information the app uses and your choices. Contact: {{EMAIL}}

## The short version
- No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
- Your profile, health details, food, exercise, steps and height logs stay on your phone.
- The optional online AI coach sends your chat messages over the internet to get a reply.

## Who can use M Grow
M Grow is for all ages, from 6 years up. People under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission, which they confirm when they set up the app. For users under 18, M Grow does not set weight-loss goals. The online AI coach is available only from age 13. Younger children see built-in quick tips that work without the internet.

## Information stored on your phone
- Profile: name (optional), age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal and diet.
- Health details you choose to add: allergies, medical conditions and notes.
- Daily logs: food, exercise, water, steps, weight and height entries, and height-habit tick marks.
- Parents' heights and a height goal, if you type them in the Height growth plan.
- Settings such as your language.

This information is kept only on your device. We do not receive it, and we cannot see or recover it. You can erase all of it any time from Me, then Delete all my data. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

## Steps
By default the app counts steps automatically. It asks for the Physical activity permission and reads your phone's step sensor. Step counts stay on your phone. You can switch counting off in the Today tab or in your phone's settings.

## Online AI coach (optional)
When you send a message to the online AI coach, the text of your recent messages is sent over the internet to our coach server, which runs on Cloudflare and uses the Meta Llama AI model on Cloudflare Workers AI to write a reply. Please do not type your full name, address, phone number or other personal details in the chat.

- Your language setting is sent so the coach can answer in it.
- Adults can choose to share a general summary: age group, goal, diet, allergies and conditions. This is off by default. Your name, exact age, height, weight and notes are never sent. For users under 18 nothing from the profile is shared.
- The coach is an AI. It can make mistakes and it is not a doctor.
- Our coach server does not save your chats. Cloudflare may keep technical request records under its own privacy policy.
- If the online coach is not available, the app shows built-in quick tips that work on the phone without sending anything.

## Feedback
When you send feedback, the app opens your email app with a draft. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app. Your email address will be visible to us when you send it.

## Health information
Allergy and condition information is sensitive. M Grow uses it only on your phone, to avoid foods you listed and to keep your plan safe. It is not sold or shared.

## Sharing
We do not sell your information. We do not show ads. We do not use analytics or advertising tools. The only data that leaves your phone is what you send to the online AI coach or by email as described above.

## Children
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app stores data only on the device, a child's profile stays with that device. Parents and guardians can delete it at any time from the Me tab. If you believe a child has sent personal information through the coach or by email, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your choices
- Delete all data on the phone: Me, then Delete all my data.
- Stop using the online AI coach: simply do not use it.
- Turn off step counting in the Today tab.

## Not medical advice
M Grow gives general wellness guidance only. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before changing your diet or exercise, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, or are under 18. Height tips in the app cannot promise a change in height.

## Changes
If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
